About me

I’m Martina, a designer from Gothenburg, Sweden.

My love for art, design, and culture has been with me since a young age. I studied graphic design for three years in high school before pursuing a bachelor’s degree at Accademia Italiana in Florence, Italy. In 2026 I began studying digital design at Yrgo, with over nine years of being a graphic designer in the bagage.

I am passionate about creating visual solutions tailored to each client’s unique needs and always strive to find the best design solution for every project, focusing on both aesthetics and functionality.
